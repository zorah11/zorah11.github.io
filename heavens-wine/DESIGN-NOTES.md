# Design direction

Palette derived from supplied posters: burgundy #410d19, deep wine #23070e, restrained gold #d7b472, cream #faf6ed, paper #f0e9db, ink #291c1c.

1. Hero: dark photographic scene, left aligned editorial headline, isolated classic bottle in the foreground.
2. Navigation: compact script-like serif Heavens wordmark, fine border, booking link. Mobile full screen menu.
3. Type: Cormorant Garamond headings with DM Sans body text. Self-hosted with font-display swap.
4. Images: transparent product cutouts; full-bleed candlelit celebration scene; portrait crop for personalised branding.
5. Rhythm: quiet introduction, interactive catalogue stage, dark event spread, branding story, enquiry form.
6. Motion: short entrance on hero text and restrained control transitions, respecting reduced motion.

Reference review: supplied Heavens posters establish all brand content and palette. Ruinart: product-first collection hierarchy. Veuve Clicquot: occasion-led editorial progression. Krug public homepage was unavailable during review. No competing brand assets or copy reused.

## Additional pages — 20 November 2026

The existing home design was approved by the client and remains the visual foundation. Four independent static pages now share the original wordmark, burgundy/gold/cream palette, self-hosted fonts, navigation and footer.

- Home retains its photographic hero, product selector and event sections. A new original-poster feature and booking invitation lead into the additional pages.
- Our Wines expands the collection with the supplied product poster and pack information.
- Celebrations brings occasion-specific enquiries and personalised branding together.
- Book an Occasion provides a dedicated form with a message review before the visitor chooses to open WhatsApp. Wine, pack and occasion choices carry across using allowlisted URL parameters.

The supplied `Heavens Wine-01.jpg.jpeg` remains unchanged in Downloads. Responsive 640px and 1400px WebP copies show the whole poster without cropping or altering its artwork. The existing bottle cutouts and generated editorial images remain in use. Company ownership is displayed as Blue Heavens Distributors Limited on every page.

The additions use `pages.css`; the original `style.css` is unchanged. No new external font, image or tracking dependencies are introduced.
