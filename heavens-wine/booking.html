What pack sizes are available?
Our advertised range includes 750 ml bottles and 1, 2, 3, 5, 10 and 20 litre packs. Contact us to confirm packaging and availability for your chosen wine.
Can you brand bottles for my event?
Yes. We offer event branding for weddings, introductions, birthdays, graduation parties and Kuhingira. Contact us to discuss your design, quantities and timing.
How do I get a price or place an order?
Call 0776 998 578 or 0709 724 471, or prepare an enquiry on our booking page. We’ll confirm pricing and fulfilment arrangements directly with you.
